System is the phone's own driver. A driver you install (a Turnip .zip made for Dolphin or Yuzu, for Snapdragon / Adreno) can be faster or fix broken graphics. Mali drivers (malisx2) run on the Vulkan renderer, which starts by itself for them. If it fails or the game closes while starting, it goes back to System by itself.	Sistem = driver bawaan HP. Gameplay	Permainan
The game keeps its own 30 fps and the phone draws an extra picture between two of its frames, so motion is smoother and the game plays exactly the same. Lite redraws only what moves (characters, doors) and moves the rest with the camera: much lighter on the GPU. Full redraws everything: about twice the GPU work. When the phone cannot keep up it drops back to 30 by itself. Smoke, fire and the menus still move at 30.	Game tetap berjalan 30 fps dan HP menggambar satu gambar tambahan di antara dua frame-nya, jadi gerakan lebih halus dan permainan sama persis. Lite hanya menggambar ulang yang bergerak (karakter, pintu) dan menggeser sisanya mengikuti kamera: jauh lebih ringan untuk GPU. Penuh menggambar ulang semuanya: kerja GPU sekitar dua kali lipat. Bila HP tidak sanggup, otomatis kembali ke 30. Asap, api, dan menu tetap bergerak 30.
